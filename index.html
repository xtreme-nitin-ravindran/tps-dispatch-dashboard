Calls by police division

TPS divisions are supplied by TPS. TFS divisions are estimated from mapped locations and postal-prefix lookups using bundled open geographic data. A postal area can span divisions; unresolved calls are Unknown.

i
For curious neighbours, not emergency decisions.

This site is only for viewing public Toronto Fire Services information and satisfying your siren-related curiosity. Your emergency plan deserves better than a browser tab. 🚒

Calls can be delayed, revised, cancelled, or excluded for privacy and operational reasons. Locations are approximate public descriptions. Do not use this dashboard to identify individuals or make decisions about your safety.

Independent and unofficial. In an emergency, call 911.
